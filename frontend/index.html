Affective Dimension Reference & Neurophysiological Significance

Valence (Hedonic Tone)

Indicates whether the emotional experience is intrinsically pleasant or unpleasant. Neurobiologically grounded in Frontal Alpha Asymmetry (FAA): greater left-frontal cortical activity reflects approach motivation and positive valence, whereas right-frontal dominance signifies withdrawal.

Arousal (Neurophysiological Activation)

Reflects bodily and mental activation from deep calmness/sleep to high excitement or panic. Correlates with cortical beta rhythm (14–30 Hz) power and event-related desynchronization across central and parietal electrodes.

Dominance (Sense of Agency)

Measures whether the subject feels empowered and in control of the situation versus submissive, overwhelmed, or helpless. Strongly tied to fronto-temporal power distributions.

Liking (Aesthetic Preference)

Reflects cognitive appraisal and personal appreciation of the auditory/visual music stimulus, modulated by reward circuitry and bilateral fronto-temporal coherence.
